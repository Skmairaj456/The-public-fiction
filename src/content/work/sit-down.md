intro: Sit Down is a sample product shoot for a furniture maker whose pieces are simple and well made — and whose old photography made them look ordinary.
challenge: Furniture photography tends to fall into two traps — sterile cut-outs or over-styled rooms. We needed images that show craft and scale and still sell the product on a busy product page.
direction: Each piece is treated as a portrait subject. Clean sets, a single bold colour against neutral walls, and light that shows the material honestly. Every hero shot has a matching detail and a matching context frame.
execution: A two-day studio shoot covering the full range, with a repeatable lighting and framing guide so future products can be shot to match by anyone.

intro: Loud, Quiet is a sample lookbook for a contemporary label whose clothes are bold but whose customers aren't trying to be noticed. We wanted pictures with the confidence of the garments and none of the posturing.
challenge: The collection mixed loud colour with quiet tailoring. Shot conventionally, one would drown the other. The images also had to work as e-commerce crops, campaign posters and vertical social content.
direction: Single saturated backgrounds, one garment colour per frame, natural poses held a beat too long. Wide frames for campaign use, with every composition planned so a 4:5 or 9:16 crop still lands.
execution: A one-day studio and location shoot with a tight shot list, followed by consistent colour grading so the whole set reads as one body of work — whichever image a customer sees first.

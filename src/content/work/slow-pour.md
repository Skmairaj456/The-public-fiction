intro: Slow Pour is a concept identity for an independent coffee house opening onto a street full of chains. The brand had to explain itself before anyone tasted anything.
challenge: Speed is what most coffee brands sell. This client wanted to sell the opposite without sounding precious or expensive — and to make a small space feel like a destination.
direction: A name that is also the promise. A warm, hand-set wordmark, a palette taken from the roast, and a tone of voice that is generous rather than clever. Photography focuses on hands and tables, never on the barista's theatre.
execution: The identity was rolled out across cups, signage, menus, a website and a launch shoot, with a guidelines document written so a new hire could understand it in ten minutes.

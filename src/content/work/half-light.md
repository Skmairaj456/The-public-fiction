intro: "Half Light introduces an independent fragrance house’s first amber scent. The idea was simple — sell a feeling of time rather than a list of notes."
challenge: "Fragrance advertising is a sea of slow-motion silk and whispered French. A small house can’t outspend that noise, so it has to out-think it. The work had to be recognisable from a single frame, with no logo in shot."
direction: "The whole world is built around the last light of the day. Warm, low, directional light; skin and glass treated with the same care; a palette that moves from amber to ink. Every asset is framed so it can be cropped to a story, a billboard or a product page without losing the idea."
execution: "One two-day shoot produced the stills, a 30-second film and a library of motion loops. Copy was kept to a single line per placement, and the system was handed over with a short guide so the in-house team could keep making work that looks like it."

intro: Half Light is a concept campaign for an independent fragrance house introducing its first amber scent. The idea was simple — sell a feeling of time rather than a list of notes.
challenge: Fragrance advertising is a sea of slow-motion silk and whispered French. A small house can't outspend that noise, so it has to out-think it. The brief asked for something people would recognise as the brand from a single frame, with no logo in shot.
direction: We built the whole world around the last light of the day. Warm, low, directional light; skin and glass treated with the same care; a palette that moves from amber to ink. Every asset was framed so it could be cropped to a story, a billboard or a product page without losing the idea.
execution: One two-day shoot produced the stills, a 30-second film and a library of motion loops. Copy was kept to a single line per placement. The system was handed over with a short guide so the in-house team could keep making work that looks like it.

intro: Field Notes is a sample social campaign for a streetwear label whose audience lives on their phones and can smell an advert from the first frame.
challenge: Polished campaign imagery was being scrolled straight past. The brand needed content that felt native to the feed without losing the craft that sets it apart.
direction: The idea was a city diary — real places, real moments, a handwritten caption system and a rule that every post must be interesting with the sound off. Shot vertical-first, with stills and motion captured together.
execution: A content plan of short series rather than one-off posts, a small library of templates for the brand's own team, and a paid layer to put the strongest pieces in front of new audiences.

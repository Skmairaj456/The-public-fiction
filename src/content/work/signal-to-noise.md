intro: Signal to Noise is a sample brand film for a specialist company in a crowded category. Instead of listing features, the film is about the discipline of saying no.
challenge: Corporate films tend to say everything and therefore nothing. The client's real strength — focus — was the hardest thing to show on camera.
direction: A script built on subtraction. Each scene removes something until only the product and the people remain. Shot on location with a small crew, warm practical light and a score that leaves room for silence.
execution: Concept, script, casting, shoot and post-production were handled end to end. The 90-second film was cut down into a 30-second spot, a set of social edits and stills pulled from the grade.
